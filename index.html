How scoring works.

Each trade gets a score from 0 to 100, computed only from its SEC filing. Purchases, larger amounts, senior roles and trades made outside a pre-set plan score higher. Sales made automatically to cover tax score zero. Trades scoring 60 or more are listed above.

Every summary on this page is assembled from the filings' own figures: rarity, size, timing and repeated trades. Nothing is estimated.
